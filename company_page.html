      About

      Company description

      Cosun Ingredients offers a broad portfolio of plant-based ingredient solutions, including Sensus® chicory root fiber, Tendra® fava bean protein and Fidesse® beet fiber. Combining expertise in fibers & prebiotics, proteins and texturants, we help food and beverage brands and manufacturers develop products that deliver on nutrition, taste, texture and sustainability. Through ingredient innovation, application expertise and scientific insights, we support the creation of future-fit food solutions that meet evolving consumer needs.
